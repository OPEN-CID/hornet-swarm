# Code of Conduct (short version)

We want useful skills from everyone, everywhere. To keep that working:

- Be kind and direct. Critique the skill, never the person.
- No harassment, hate speech, spam, or plagiarism. Submitted skills must be
  your own work or compatibly licensed.
- Medical, legal, and financial skills must stay informational and carry the
  standard disclaimer — never diagnose, dose, or conclude.
- Maintainers may edit, rename, or decline submissions to protect quality;
  decisions are final but will be explained.

Problems? Open an issue titled `[conduct]` or contact a maintainer privately
via a GitHub security advisory. Deliberate harm (malicious instructions,
exfiltrated secrets) means an immediate ban and revert.
